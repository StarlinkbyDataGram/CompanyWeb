It says new customers from 14 July 2026, and existing customers from 17 August 2026: Roam Unlimited allows up to 30 days outside the registered home country at a time; other Roam plans are home-country (or grouped region) only; Local Priority allows up to 60 days total international use; Global Priority has no time restriction and covers ocean use.
